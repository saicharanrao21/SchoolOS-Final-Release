# Attendance Intelligence

SchoolOS attendance now includes operational attendance intelligence on top of the existing session/record/correction workflow.

## Capabilities
- School daily attendance summary by session/class/section
- Present, late, absent and overall attendance-rate metrics
- Student attendance risk identification
- Configurable attendance threshold (default 75%)
- Consecutive-absence detection (3+ days medium risk, 5+ days high risk)
- Server-side organization and school ownership validation
- Student statistics cannot cross organization/school boundaries

The endpoints are intended to feed management dashboards and future automated parent/student attendance alerts without changing the canonical attendance-record model.
